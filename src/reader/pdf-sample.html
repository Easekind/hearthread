The Architecture
of Reading

An original sample essay / November 2026

Space is part of the sentence.

A reading interface is a place to spend time. Its structure should help the eye find the next line, and help the mind stay with the thought.

Find a book / Find your place / Keep reading

A library gives things a place. It makes a growing collection understandable without asking every book to compete for attention. A cover can provide a useful landmark; a quiet list can make a larger collection easier to navigate.

The reading view has a different responsibility. Here, controls should be available without becoming the subject. The width of a line, the rhythm between paragraphs, and the contrast of the page all contribute to the experience.

Returning to a book should feel continuous. A reader remembers a sentence, a chapter, or a passage. Page numbers change when type changes. The place in the text should remain.

Good defaults are a form of care. They make the first page comfortable while leaving room for people to choose what works best for them.

Keep the essential things close.

Search, contents, and typography belong within reach. Everything else can wait until the book is closed.
